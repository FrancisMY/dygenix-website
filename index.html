Project work

Field-validated QR Scanner workflow support for an industrial process.

Recent validation

From manual checking to automatic scan validation.

Challenge: manual delivery validation caused delays and risk of missed scans. Operators had to scan and check delivery notes by hand, then verify whether all goods were scanned correctly.

Solution: Dygenix built and validated a Keyence-based QR Scanner solution with a custom operator dashboard. When goods pass through the scan zone, the system reads the codes automatically.

Result: the system is commissioned, API-ready and flexible enough to keep growing. It can scan up to 8 codes per second on moving goods and detect codes from up to 4 meters away.

Urgent support

Production stopped?

Downtime is not an abstract word. When a machine stops, the priority is to understand what changed, what is failing and what can be done safely.

Dygenix helps identify faults in:

  • PLC and control logic
  • HMI workflows
  • Field communication
  • Industrial data and logging
  • CANBUS / J1939 networks

Goal

Controlled analysis, clear communication and restoring reliable operation quickly and safely.

Approach

Start with the process. Then write the software.

01

Understand the machine

Look at the process, operators, safety assumptions and failure modes before touching the code.

02

Build the smallest reliable path

Make the critical behavior work first. Then harden it with tests, logging and clear state.

03

Leave it maintainable

Document what matters, isolate the logic and keep future troubleshooting practical.
